Hi, I'm Drew

Sat down at five. Never got up.

I'm a full-time professional musician in Salt Lake City — keys and voice, for over a decade. I play more than 8,000 songs from memory: no sheet music, no setlist, no screen in front of me. Name it and I play it. That's the whole act.

Everybody else in this town is reading lyrics and chords off an iPad. I don't need one — and nobody in Salt Lake does this better.

I'm an improviser, too. Give me a title, a mood, or somebody's name and there's a song that didn't exist a second ago.

I sat down at a piano at five and never got up. At sixteen I won a national classical championship, and Westminster answered with a full ride. About 1,300 shows have happened since.

I want every eye in the room. Packed house or one guy at the Star Bar in Park City — doesn't matter.

The Bar Show

Someone yells for Free Bird.

Usually that's a heckle. Here it's just a request — and nine minutes later the room is still on its feet with their beers in the air, while the bachelorette party in the corner orders another round of shots.

Everybody left their worries at the door somewhere in the middle of the solo.

Photo: Packed bar, crowd singing, beers up

How the night runs

  • The crowd picks every song. No setlist, ever. The whole show is dictated by what that room wants to hear.
  • They tip to jump the queue. Requests compete. The better the night gets, the more they spend to hear what's next.
  • 8,000+ songs from memory. Every other player in town is reading lyrics and chords off a screen. I'm not — whatever they yell, I already know it.
  • Live karaoke on tap. When the room wants to sing it themselves, I back them live.
  • The magic trick. Once a night I learn a song I've never heard and play it back perfectly.

What I'm looking for

One room, one night a week. I've held a weekly bar night for ten years — four hours a night, three and four nights a week. I know how to carry a slow Tuesday, and how to still be carrying it in month eleven.

Short bio — copy this for listings and event pages

Drew is a full-time professional pianist and vocalist in Salt Lake City, known as the Human Jukebox, performing more than 8,000 songs entirely from memory. He never uses sheet music and never brings a setlist — every song is a request. He is also an improviser, composing music on the spot. A national champion of the Stillman Kelley classical competition and a Westminster music graduate, he has played more than 1,300 shows, released two albums and an EP, and spent ten years playing weekly dueling pianos in Salt Lake City. He is a regular improv pianist for Improv Broadway, Crowdsourced Comedy, and Salt Lake Improv, and plays bar residencies, weddings, private parties, and live karaoke.
