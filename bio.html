Drew

Pianist and vocalist. Salt Lake City, Utah.

Drew is a full-time professional musician. He has worked full time in music for over a decade.

He performs more than 8,000 songs from memory. He does not use sheet music and does not work from a setlist. The audience requests songs and he plays them. Most other players in the market read lyrics and chords off a screen.

He also improvises. Given a title, a subject, or a name, he composes and performs original music on the spot. He works regularly as an improv comedy pianist.

He plays solo with a loop machine, which fits a small stage and produces a full-band sound. He has played more than 1,300 shows.
