More About Drew

[Photo: portrait at the piano, looking at camera]

I sat down at a piano when I was five and never really got up.

At sixteen I won a national classical piano championship, which turned into a full-tuition scholarship and a degree in theory and piano performance. Classical training is where I learned to hear something once and keep it.

Then I went to work. Ten years of weekly dueling pianos in Salt Lake City, four hours a night, three and four nights a week. A couple of years as band director and pianist for The Drifters, which taught me I'd rather run my own show than manage anybody else's. Somewhere in there, thirteen hundred shows.

These days I'm on the roster at three improv companies, playing Park City rooms where the crowd is different every night, and writing songs that go around locally online.

What I'm actually after is real-world community. Music is how people process the stress of ordinary life, and a good night gives them somewhere to put it. I want a room where you can belt your favorite song, cheers the birthday girl, and laugh at how ridiculous all of it is.

Short bio — copy this for listings and event pages

Drew is a Salt Lake City pianist and vocalist who performs more than 8,000 songs from memory, without sheet music or a setlist.
