The Bar Show

Someone yells for Free Bird.

Usually that's a heckle. Here it's a request. Nine minutes later the room is still on its feet, and the bachelorette party in the corner is ordering another round.

[Photo: packed room, crowd engaged]

How the night runs

  • The crowd picks every song. No setlist. The whole show is whatever that room wants to hear.
  • They tip to jump the queue. Requests compete. People who want to hear their song next stay, and they keep ordering.
  • Everything from memory. 8,000+ songs, no sheet music, no screen. Most players in this town read lyrics and chords off an iPad.
  • Live karaoke on tap. When the room wants to sing it themselves, I back them live.

What I'm looking for

One room, one night a week. I've held a weekly bar night for ten years. Four hours a night, three and four nights a week. I know how to carry a slow Tuesday, and how to still be carrying it in month eleven.

More About Drew

[Photo: portrait at the piano, looking at camera]

I've worked full time in music for over a decade. I play piano and sing, and I take requests all night without sheet music or a setlist.

I improvise too. Give me a title, a subject, or somebody's name and I'll write the song on the spot. That's most of what I do with the improv comedy groups here.

I started piano at five. At sixteen I won the Stillman Kelley national classical championship, and Westminster gave me a full ride. I've played more than 1,300 shows since.

Packed house or one guy at the Star Bar in Park City, I want every eye in the room.

What's in the hands

Raised on the Carpenters, ABBA, Styx, Boston, Queen, Dan Fogelberg, and at nine years old, NSYNC.

Shaped by Beethoven, Billy Joel, John Williams, and Jon Schmidt.

Sharpened by Dr. Dre, Tupac, Eminem, and Bo Burnham.

Credentials

  • National Champion, Stillman Kelley classical piano competition, 2008. Won at sixteen.
  • Sterling Scholar. Full-tuition scholarship to Westminster.
  • B.A. Music, Westminster. Theory and piano performance.
  • Ten years of dueling pianos, weekly, at the Tavernacle in Salt Lake City.
  • The Drifters. Band director, synth, and piano.
  • Two albums and an EP released on Spotify.

Now

  • Regular improv pianist on three rosters: Improv Broadway, Crowdsourced Comedy, and Salt Lake Improv.
  • Improv comedy musicals at WhyKiki and a vegan diner downtown.
  • The Spur in Park City. The crowd is mostly out-of-state and changes nightly.
  • Après-ski at the St. Regis. Recently the Murray Theatre.
  • The Gateway tree lighting ceremony, every year.
  • Original songs that circulate locally on social media.

Short bio — copy this for listings and event pages

Drew is a Salt Lake City pianist and vocalist who performs more than 8,000 songs from memory, without sheet music or a setlist. He is a national champion of the Stillman Kelley classical piano competition and a Westminster music graduate. He has played over 1,300 shows, released two albums and an EP, and spent ten years playing weekly dueling pianos in Salt Lake City. He is a regular improv pianist for Improv Broadway, Crowdsourced Comedy, and Salt Lake Improv.
